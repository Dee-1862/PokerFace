
# Unified Poker AR System Instructions

## How to Save Your Hand (Register Hand)
1. **Fan your cards** so they are clearly visible to the camera.
2. Raise your **LEFT HAND** and make a **PINCH** gesture (Touch Thumb & Index finger tips together).
3. **HOLD** this pinch for **4 seconds**.
   - You will see a "Pinch & Hold" loading indicator on your hand.
4. When the indicator completes (locks), the system will scan for the 2 largest/clearest cards.
5. It will save them as "My Hand" (highlighted in Green).
6. You can now put your cards down.

## How to View Stats
1. Raise your **RIGHT HAND** and Pinch.
2. **Move your hand vertically** (Stretch/Slide) to expand the AR stats panel.

## Troubleshooting
- If the system detects "8h" instead of "8c" (or vice versa), ensure good lighting.
- If the "King" is missed, try angling it slightly.
- The system is currently using `poker_v1.pt`. If you have `poker_v2.pt`, rename it and restart for better accuracy.
